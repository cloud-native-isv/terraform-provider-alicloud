## Overview

## Implementation Notes

- Keep naming boundary explicit: legacy behavior under `alicloud_logtail_config`, new capability under `alicloud_logtail_pipeline_config`.
- Plan artifacts are located under `.specify/specs/004-logtail-pipeline-config/` and `.specify/specs/005-restore-logtail-config/`.
